How to use

      

        Drag to move, pinch to zoom, double-tap to zoom in. Tap a tile for its details.

        Change input amounts under Inputs, then Calculate (or ▶ on the map).
        A red →X next to an input means only X of it actually goes in.

        Guess in/out belts: pretend marker arms on edge belts where items seem to come in or go out.
        Tap one (or its belt) and use the button on its card to switch it off; dashed outlines are possible
        ones that are switched off — tap them to switch them on.

        Outlines: input, output,
        at max speed, limits the output,
        items wait, jam.
